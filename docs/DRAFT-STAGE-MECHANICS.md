# DRAFT: Stage-Specific Mechanics (TO BE REVIEWED)

*This document contains brainstorming ideas for the early stages of Holoverse. These are not yet finalized or approved for implementation.*

---

## 1. The Cosmic Stage: "The Resource Miner"
**Role:** Raw Material Supplier.
**Core Gameplay:**
*   **Fine-Tuning:** Players optimize the fundamental laws of their universe (gravity, electromagnetism, etc.).
*   **Production:** Efficient tuning leads to the generation of **Exotic Matter** (e.g., Stardust, Dark Matter, Antimatter).
**Economics:**
*   These materials are required by Social-stage universes for high-tech infrastructure (Teleporters, Reactors).
*   Cosmic players sell these materials to advanced players for $NRG, providing immediate economic utility upon minting.

## 2. The Biological Stage: "The Genetic Architect"
**Role:** IP/Asset Creator.
**Core Gameplay:**
*   **Directed Evolution:** Players guide species through mutation and natural selection.
*   **Trait Engineering:** Optimize for specific traits (e.g., resilience, aesthetic rarity, intelligence).
**Economics:**
*   Social-stage owners buy "Genetic Seeds" to populate their worlds with diverse and useful species.
*   Biological players can "patent" a species and sell its tokenized blueprint on the marketplace.
